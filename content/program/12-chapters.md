# The 12 chapters — working draft

Course, program, book, twelve weeks, twelve months. The container is decided
later. The chapters are buckets for now: put dumps and sources in, see where
the weight lands, rename as it becomes clear.

The theme underneath all of it: **how to rebuild your life after trauma, or
after losing everything.** Self-mastery in practice.

Mirrored in `app/src/lib/program.ts` — change both, or ask Claude to.

| # | Title | The question it answers |
|---|-------|-------------------------|
| 1 | Ground zero | What does starting over actually look like, and why is it not the end? |
| 2 | The story you tell yourself | How do I notice, and then change, the thoughts that run my life? |
| 3 | Clear head | Why a sober lifestyle, and how do I make it the default rather than the fight? |
| 4 | One day at a time | How do I build discipline that survives bad days? |
| 5 | Order | How do I organise my life when everything feels like chaos? |
| 6 | The money problem | How do I face the finances I have been avoiding? |
| 7 | Deciding | How do I make decisions I will not have to undo? |
| 8 | Planning without fantasy | How do I plan in a way that respects reality? |
| 9 | Getting rid of problems | How do I actually close things instead of carrying them? |
| 10 | People | Who stays, who goes, and how do I ask for help? |
| 11 | Self-mastery | What does it mean to be in charge of myself? |
| 12 | Building again | How do I build something new on the ground I have cleared? |

## Notes

- Topics named so far, in Giulia's words: self-mastery, managing your thoughts,
  sober lifestyle, being disciplined, organising your life, getting rid of
  problems (financial and otherwise), making decisions, planning.
- Chapters 1 and 12 are the frame. 2–11 are the work. If it ends up being ten
  chapters of work and a beginning and an end, that is fine.
- Same twelve serve as the content-strategy spine: each chapter is a month of
  posts, emails, and a lead magnet.
