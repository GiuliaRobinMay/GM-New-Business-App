# The brief — as of 2026-09-17

In Giulia's words, lightly ordered. This is the starting point; everything
else in the repo serves it.

## What it is

A program people can join. Twelve steps — weeks, months, decided later. Also
a book with twelve chapters. The same twelve chapters are the spine of the
content strategy and the marketing.

## What it is about

How to rebuild your life after trauma, or after losing everything. Self-mastery:

- managing your thoughts
- a sober lifestyle
- discipline
- organising your life
- getting rid of your problems — financial and otherwise
- making decisions, planning

The authority comes from having done it, more than once.

## What it is not

Not connected to the current day job. Separate audience, separate voice,
separate library.

## First functionality

1. Brain dumps, from anywhere — phone, or talking at the computer. Daily.
2. A place to gather books and other sources that shaped the thinking.
3. Then, together with Claude: work the material, find the tone of voice,
   build the business out from there.

## Open questions

- Name of the program / book.
- Twelve weeks or twelve months.
- Where the program is delivered (community platform, email course, both).
- Who exactly it is for — the first reader.
